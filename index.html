How It Works

١

WhatsApp Lesson

The teacher starts a WhatsApp call or group with students. WhatsApp handles all lesson audio — the website does not touch your microphone or camera.

٢

Open the Student Reader

Students open this website on their phone browsers while listening on WhatsApp. The authentic Arabic Quran text is displayed in a clean, distraction-free layout.

٣

Teacher Controls the Lesson

The teacher logs in to the Dashboard on a laptop, selects the Surah and Ayah, then starts the lesson. As the teacher recites, they advance the word marker.

٤

Real-Time Word Underline

Every connected student's screen updates instantly — the current word is underlined so students can always follow exactly where the teacher is reciting.

📱 About WhatsApp & This Website

This website works alongside WhatsApp — it does not replace it and cannot control it. WhatsApp remains your audio communication tool. This website adds live Quran text synchronisation so students can follow the text on their screens.

Students should keep their WhatsApp microphone muted (unless asked to recite) using WhatsApp's own mute button.
